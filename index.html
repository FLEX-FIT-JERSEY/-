Customized

Bring your own concept and we will shape it into teamwear that feels like yours.

If your team already has an idea, logo, or mockup, upload it to Google Drive and paste the link in the order form. Use notes for color direction, sleeve style, sponsor position, and any extra instructions.

Custom order flow

  1. Choose a shirt or kit design.
  2. Open its big product screen.
  3. Click order and fill customer details first.
  4. Go next and fill every player row according to quantity.
  5. Submit through Formspree and receive replies by email.

What you can customize

  • Player name for each shirt
  • Player number for each shirt
  • Player size from S to XXL
  • Google Drive design link
  • Extra details in notes
